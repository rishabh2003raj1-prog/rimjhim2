Privacy policy

How Rimjhim Cafe handles personal data in its cafe management app and customer accounts.

Who we are

Rimjhim Cafe, Sector 27, Noida ("we", "the cafe") runs this app to manage the cafe: billing, stock, purchases, staff and customer accounts. For any question about your data, speak to the cafe owner at the counter or on the cafe's WhatsApp number.

What we collect

  • Customers: your name and phone number, the bills recorded under your name, udhaar (credit) and payments, and visits. If you use your customer account, we also store a scrambled copy of your access code.
  • Delivery orders: name, phone number and address, used to deliver the order.
  • Staff: name, role, shifts, attendance, pay and payments, and login details (passwords are stored only in scrambled form).
  • Partners and business records: sales, purchases, expenses, cash, stock and similar records of the business.
  • Technical: error reports from the app (the error message, the screen and the phone's browser type) so problems can be fixed.

Why we use it

  • To bill orders, deliver food, keep udhaar balances and loyalty visits, and show customers their own account.
  • To run the cafe: pay and roster staff, keep accounts, stock and purchase records, and meet legal and tax obligations.
  • To keep the app working and secure.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising by others. We may send you a WhatsApp message (for example an udhaar reminder or an offer) only from the cafe's own phone.

Who can see it

  • Each staff login sees only what its job needs; pay, partner and bank details are limited to the owner and partners.
  • A customer account shows only that customer's own records.
  • The app is hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), which stores the data on our behalf; its servers may be outside India. We do not share data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Business and tax records are kept as long as the law requires (generally up to 8 years for accounts). Customer details are kept while you are a customer or have an udhaar balance; you can ask us to delete them once nothing is owed. Daily backups are kept for 14 days.

Your rights

In line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it (where we don't need to keep it by law), and you can withdraw consent for messages at any time. Ask the cafe owner; we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords and access codes are stored only in scrambled form. Every login has its own role, and the server decides what each one may see or change. Signing out clears the data from the phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
